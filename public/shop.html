The HuntClock difference

HuntClock is a wind-up, battery-free dog enrichment game. A sealed clockwork timer releases treats every 20–30 minutes across 1, 2 or 3 hours into six different puzzle stations: Flap Nudge, Rope Pull, Sniff Tunnel, Roll Wheel, Slide Lid and Lick Pocket. A soft bell dings each time a station unlocks, so your dog learns to come back and hunt.

Normal puzzle toys are finished in 5–15 minutes and dogs memorise the solution. HuntClock keeps your dog busy for up to 3 hours, and swappable puzzle discs (Easy / Medium / Genius) rearrange the stations so your dog can never memorise the answer.

Specifications

  • Power: wind-up clockwork timer (no batteries)
  • Timer intervals: every 20–30 minutes
  • Duration: 1, 2 or 3 hours
  • Stations: 6 (Flap Nudge, Rope Pull, Sniff Tunnel, Roll Wheel, Slide Lid, Lick Pocket)
  • Discs: swappable Easy / Medium / Genius
  • Cleaning: parts detach, dishwasher-safe
  • Base: weighted with optional anchor strap
  • Sizes: S (up to 10 kg), M (10–25 kg), L (25 kg+)

Safety & care

  • Chew-safe materials tested for Indian dogs
  • All parts detach and are dishwasher-safe
  • Weighted base resists tipping; optional anchor strap for extra stability
  • No small batteries or electronics that can be swallowed
  • Supervise your dog the first few times
  • Wipe clean with a damp cloth between washes

What's in the box

  • 1 HuntClock unit (size of your choice)
  • 1 puzzle disc (Easy, Medium or Genius)
  • 6 station inserts (Flap Nudge, Rope Pull, Sniff Tunnel, Roll Wheel, Slide Lid, Lick Pocket)
  • 1 anchor strap (optional)
